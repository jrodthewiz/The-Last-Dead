# Blackwater map craft pass — 23 October 2026

The current Three.js browser game received 100 distinct subagent assignments in bounded batches (at most three children at once): five implementation owners and 95 focused source, capture and release reviews. Unity was outside this pass. Shared captures and explicit ownership prevented competing writers and duplicate browser workloads. These are analytical reviews, not claims of independent human playtests or Nintendo production parity.

## Implemented

- Four authored island route compositions: the Western Trail fork marker, keeper boundary, nurse log and Briar tool rest. Markers have readable front/back faces with consistent world directions.
- Three household work corners: arrival drywood, keeper rope repair, orchard harvest crate and pot. Footings sample the actual terrain triangles.
- Distinct press, radio, shipping and architecture forecourts, plus a hospital triage trolley. Large forms share collision bounds; doors remain clear.
- Ninety city interiors now distinguish active work, proofs, packing, offline equipment and handover logs. This removes 2,752 repeated interior pieces rather than filling every surface.
- Civic beds, benches, maintenance corner, waiting shelter, connected approach, tower bypass and a separate refuge. The save fire was moved out of the clock base. The hospital fascia and relay were moved off the entrance axis.
- Four localized roadside fern colonies, preserving quiet open ground.
- Shared support for landing on new furniture, including capsule clearance and spatial caching. Civic save recovery handles foot saves, occupied vehicles and invalid vehicle entries without discarding progress.

## Evidence and limits

All 248 entrances were sampled over 21 positions (5,208 checks). Selected ordinary-keyboard walking legs covered the civic bypass, bridge, hospital, business entries and all three named homes. The initial shelter approaches failed against the obelisk and bench; the repaired branch/side/pad legs all reached their targets. Four representative new furniture surfaces passed landing and walk-off checks. Five destination stashes retain stable IDs and their existing weapon rewards.

Eleven existing scavenging/craft checks pass. Three other existing checks still fail: a straight route into an apartment divider, an old sink role assertion, and an enemy fixture missing getObjectByName. The identical failures were reproduced against HEAD versions of the changed source modules without adding files or changing the checks. They are not reported as passes.

Root runtime, local dist, Sites public and Sites client build matched across all 11 changed/new runtime files (33 normalized comparisons). Captures and inline runners remain in the ignored output/map-craft-2026-10-23 folder. No test files were added or modified.

Final review accepts the bounded map-craft improvement. Remaining design risks are western-route continuity at ordinary eye height, office rooms whose function still depends heavily on signs, and performance headroom on other hardware. Still captures do not prove complete traversal, horror impact, or a full multiplayer soak. Player acceptance remains open.

## Continuous traversal observation

A 67.7-second continuous local keyboard traversal completed 17 connected civic/shelter legs with no captured errors. 3,978 animation frames measured median 16.7 ms, p95 16.8 ms and one 133.3 ms spike. Packaging was running during that sample; this is not evidence of headroom on other hardware or a zero-hitch release. The guarded browser never acquired native pointer lock.
