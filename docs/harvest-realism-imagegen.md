# G harvest image texture and relief pass

The native built-in ImageGen tool generated a four-region tissue albedo and an aligned grayscale height edit. These are generated material references, not calibrated biological scans. The prior orthographic image-to-UV experiment in `scripts/bake-projection-texture.py` informed the approach: runtime art lives in mesh UV space, with no camera-projected decal. The organ geometry and actual source-model incision still supply their own silhouettes and depth.

Runtime assets:

- `assets/textures/harvest-tissue-atlas-v1.webp`: 1024², sRGB albedo; regions muscle/heart, pale serosa, liver/lung, bone.
- `assets/textures/harvest-tissue-height-v1.webp`: 1024², linear scalar relief, used for bounded parallax, derivative micro-normal and wet roughness.

The full-resolution native PNGs are preserved under `.art-source/harvest-realism/`. `scripts/prepare-harvest-textures.mjs` only copies, resizes and converts formats. The game requires neither Sharp nor Python; these are offline conversion utilities.

Albedo prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: production PBR albedo texture atlas for close-up procedural internal anatomy in a realistic horror video game. Generate a square 2048 by 2048 image containing EXACTLY four equal 1024-square seamless material texture tiles in a clean 2x2 grid, edge-to-edge with no borders, gutters, labels, text or objects. TOP LEFT: deep oxblood red muscular tissue with fine interwoven fibers, small branching capillaries and delicate connective tissue, muted naturally varied reds. TOP RIGHT: pale muted rose-pink visceral serosa with delicate blue-red branching veins, translucent-looking fibrous membranes, fine organic folds and subtle mottled warm fat flecks, suitable for intestine and stomach skin. BOTTOM LEFT: dark burgundy liver/lung capsule material, fine lobular mottling, very small branching vessels and naturally uneven matte-wet biological surface, rich restrained maroons. BOTTOM RIGHT: old warm ivory bone with fine porous trabecular grain, tiny weathering pits, slight brownish discoloration, anatomical bone rather than wood or fabric. Every tile is a flat orthographic macro material scan filling its whole quadrant, neutral diffuse cross-polarized lighting, no baked shadows, no specular highlights, no perspective, no organs or whole body silhouettes, no instruments. Photographic realism and fine material detail, materially different tiles, no cartoon or plastic aesthetic. All four tiles must tile periodically at their own left/right and top/bottom boundaries. This is a reusable surface texture atlas, not a gore scene or anatomy diagram.

Height edit prompt (using the generated albedo as its sole target):

> Edit the supplied tissue material atlas into a precise registered grayscale HEIGHT/DISPLACEMENT MAP for its original pixels. Keep EXACTLY the same square canvas, 2x2 tile divisions, feature placement, fiber directions, fold positions, pore patterns, capillary positions and scale. This is a technical texture map, not a new painting. Remove all color and all directional illumination. Mid grey 50% represents neutral surface height, darker values recessed capillaries and pores, lighter values raised muscle fibers, membrane folds and bone ridges. Top-left muscle fibers have subtle flowing relief and branching capillary grooves. Top-right serosa has delicate shallow folded ridges and recessed vessels, with small soft raised fat flecks. Bottom-left capsule has shallow small porous indentations and fine vessels. Bottom-right bone pores are recessed dark and bony ridges are raised pale grey. Retain realistic varying height, with most tissue near middle grey, no directional highlight/shadow gradients, no large artificial craters, no text/borders, no RGB tint, no new feature locations. Every feature should register directly to the input albedo for runtime parallax and derivative normals. Preserve seamless tile boundary patterns as closely as possible.

Technique: `extraction-tissue-materials.js` borrows two shared images for all harvested corpses. Its eight-step parallax ray is bounded at grazing angles; UV derivatives use the unwrapped tube/organ coordinates. Five tile-clamped taps filter the atlas without using an unpadded mip chain. Relief fades under minification and modifies, rather than replaces, the authored curved normal. Incident radiance is bounded before the light-normal term and BRDF, replacing the old post-light clipping that flattened the organs. The cavity and posterior organs have explicit recessed gain/occlusion. Diagnostic views expose albedo, height, roughness, normal and region IDs.

Other authored changes: curved tapered thorax ribs and fractured anterior ends; deeper irregular cavity; organ volumes at varied depths; irregular gut coiling; three true source-seam tissue bands; distributed compliant mesentery forces and progressively released coil support; fitted scalpel and forceps with restrained steel response. The shared zombie GLB and skeleton remain intact, and the actual private source torso still splits/retracts along its incision.

Visual review revisions: the first textured capture still contained oversized, overlit legacy blood spheres and a gut endpoint pulled 30 cm upward. Those were removed from the actual-model presentation. Blood is now 1.5–3 mm ballistic droplets; syringe blood stays inside its fitted chamber. Bowel spills sideways through an optional dynamic spring guide and releases early instead of being teleported along the heart pull. Incident-light ceiling is 2.4, with serosa diffuse gain 0.70 and bone 0.80. The seam varies in thickness and has darker recessed fascia. The shortened forceps have hooked separated jaws, grip width fitted to the current heart, and a visible jaw plane.

QA isolation: `scripts/prepare-harvest-qa.mjs` copies the build's explicit runtime file/directory whitelist into a private `.codex-temp/harvest-production-PID` snapshot. This avoids unrelated builds deleting shared `dist` during browser review. Set `EXTRACTION_CUTAWAY_ROOT` to that saved absolute path. The browser harness still uses its own ephemeral port and disposable Chrome. `HARVEST_REALISM_QA=1` checks image readiness, seam layers, physical contour bounds, tool grip and blood phase gating, and records a separate uninterrupted held-G journey after the standard settings/cancellation/cleanup checks. Its deliberate 17 cm torso-teleport probe allows solver time before checking the dynamic tissue contour.

Completed verification: 85 targeted checks and the production build pass. `output/playwright/harvest-realism-v6/report.json` passes all 18 browser views with zero errors; `runtime-integrity.json` verifies eight harvest modules/assets match the canonical source. The independent visual agent accepted final stills and sampled uninterrupted motion. Gut spill settles behind the near flap quickly, so its external motion is less readable than heart handling; corpse reaction is subtle. These assets improve the authored game anatomy and cosmetic physical constraints. They are generated material references rather than calibrated anatomical scans or a biological soft-body model.

Skill ledger: native imagegen with its prompting reference; narrow game-director orchestration; procedural geometry with its profile-sweep and gallery references; procedural materials with procedural-pbr-system and gallery example; existing gameplay/physics-selection and QA/visual/playtest/release references. Existing generated character, arm and steel assets were retained. No external model service or paid API integration was needed for this bounded texture/geometry pass.
