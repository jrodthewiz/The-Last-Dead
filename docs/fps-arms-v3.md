### Equipment and hold contract

`assets/survivor/fps-grip-profiles.js` defines the present eight equipment profiles and anatomical wrist limits. The five fitting families are `pistol`, `fore-end`, `vertical`, `shaft`, and `handle`. These select a Blender fitting method; they do not replace a fitted mesh or supply a runtime universal grasp. The named assembly and four Actions in the GLB must match `FIRST_PERSON_HOLD_POSES[index].name`. The public actual sockets remain `grip` and `supportGrip`, returned by `_findWeaponSocket`; the complete live socket frame drives each arm after clip playback. Injury roots remain `group.userData.viewArms.rightArm` / `leftArm`.

For future equipment, a pistol normally uses right `pistol` with no support arm, a rifle/sniper uses right `pistol` plus left `fore-end`, and axe/hammer/pipe/tire-iron handles use `shaft` as appropriate for each hand. Chainsaw uses right `pistol` and left `handle`. Add the actual equipment geometry and sockets first, author its own contact pose/Actions in Blender, then add its named assembly to the runtime export and test the full animation. Existing Mourning and energy weapons intentionally use vertical support handles. New equipment should not be assigned an unrelated existing weapon index just to obtain arms.
