# The Last Dead — classic game interface review

The interface now uses Blackwater's skull and tide imagery, engraved serif menus, a physical metal field case, and paper records. The HUD keeps survival information in the corners and leaves the world visible. The player promise is: **Survive Blackwater and carry its recovered records home.**

Three design pillars guide the result: the world remains the main image; equipment and records feel like objects from Blackwater; health, the current weapon, and the next objective remain readable. In conflicts, survival signals and actions come first, navigation second, and lore decoration third.

The health number now accompanies a skull and tide dial whose arc follows actual health, including critical health. The desktop weapon readout shows one large current weapon symbol. The title, pause, and recovery menus use a diamond selection cursor and illuminated text. Equipment selection, the selected weapon's details, and its fitting action share the desktop field-case view. The journal uses warm paper and dark body text.

The skull seal is original SVG artwork. Metal and leather use existing bundled game textures. No external game artwork was copied. The CSS layer is included in the production build; the life dial is updated by the existing HUD state.

## Research and readability

Classic survival-horror interfaces informed the physical case, paper records, restrained color, and text selection language. The original Resident Evil 4 press-kit [inventory archive](https://www.mobygames.com/game/16373/resident-evil-4/promo/group-4992/image-59486/) was a reference lead. Its remote image could not be opened for pixel inspection, so it does not establish visual equivalence. Our own before/after captures are the comparison evidence.

Microsoft's [XAG 101 text display guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/101) informed text hierarchy and readability. Its [XAG 102 contrast guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/102) informed the local dark backing behind HUD text and the opaque paper journal. This pass does not claim complete XAG compliance: small secondary labels, configurable text scaling, and contrast across every moving background still need a broader accessibility pass.

## Focused review lenses

The primary assistant applied the three saved profiles separately. This side conversation prohibits subagents, so these are analytical lenses applied by one assistant, not independent judge-agent results or human playtests. The method follows the repository's [adversarial review contract](C:/Users/wolfk/Desktop/thelastdead/docs/adversarial-review/README.md).

### Ruthless creative director

**Strongest objection — RISK:** materials and a skull can still be a cosmetic reskin if the interface has no consistent relationship to the game's promise. Do not add ornaments or dashboard panels to every surface.

**Evidence:** the current HUD uses the skull and tide life dial; the kit repeats the seal and material; the journal presents recovered records as paper. The desktop case puts the equipped weapon and fitting decision beside its inventory. These patterns connect to survival, scavenging, and recovered evidence. See the comparison above and [game-interface.css](C:/Users/wolfk/Desktop/thelastdead/game-interface.css).

**Counterevidence:** the HUD retains small utilitarian labels, and the field kit still exposes a regular selection grid. Those support navigation, but distinctiveness is not established by implementation or one viewer's approval.

**Experiment that changes the verdict:** show five unfamiliar viewers the HUD and field kit for five seconds each. At least four should describe a survival-horror game and connect the case or records to scavenging/recovery, without a genre prompt. Stop adding decoration until this succeeds. A poor result would justify revising the motifs before increasing their detail.

### Impatient outsider

**Strongest objection — UNVERIFIED:** a player can still miss the first safehouse objective or become overloaded by the long optional controls guide. A polished screenshot cannot prove that a newcomer knows what to do.

**Evidence:** the first objective remains explicit; B / Field Kit and street-route guidance are present; controls are disclosed on demand. Resume and checkpoint return now remain visible without scrolling at the tested sizes. The short landscape recovery view puts checkpoint information and actions beside the message.

**Counterevidence:** reading all input bindings still takes time. The thin secondary text may be difficult at a TV viewing distance. No novice observation or physical phone session was performed.

**Experiment that changes the verdict:** five first-time players start without coaching. At least four should identify the safehouse objective and open the field map within two minutes. Observe wrong actions and first hesitation; revise wording or hierarchy around that evidence.

### Validation gate

**Strongest objection:** successful layouts must not be promoted into a full game release claim. A frozen scene cannot certify combat readability, runtime frame rate, co-op, or touch hardware.

**Verdict — UNVERIFIED for full game readiness:** the bounded UI checks support the layout and action claims. The layout fixture covers 13 states at six sizes, including narrow portrait and short landscape. It checks horizontal fit, button targets, settings row separation, mission clipping, life-dial values, and visible primary menu actions. Keyboard callbacks and settings focus handling are checked. The separate camera fixture checks action labels, telemetry fit, and help/resume/restart behavior at six sizes. The below-fold landscape recovery action and decorative cursor contaminating accessible button names were confirmed defects during this pass; both were fixed and rechecked.

**Counterevidence:** the built-game UI journey starts the real game and uses real inputs, but deliberately holds its world frame after startup. It does not exercise pointer capture because the existing webdriver path skips pointer lock. Co-op disclosure layout is covered, while connecting peers is outside this pass.

**Experiment that changes the verdict:** exercise the HUD in moving combat at full and critical health against bright and dark environments, then repeat inventory and recovery on a physical phone and with a controller. Any obscured objective, unclear state, or blocked action would reopen the corresponding UI decision.
