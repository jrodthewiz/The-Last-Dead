## Proposed translation to The Last Dead

The following are our design proposals, not claims about specific events in the film:

- Short-range cold illumination with deliberate falloff, readable immediate footing and uncertain distant depth.
- Large uninterrupted areas of darkness with local, layered haze. Mist should have gaps and movement; avoid a flat visibility-reducing overlay.
- Almost-human figures in ordinary decayed clothing. Stillness, an inappropriate expression, and partial concealment supply unease.
- A single distant muted red source, replacing the repeated bright red grid as the primary supernatural cue.
- Let existing industrial architecture feel abandoned and impossible to fully see. Additional decoration is secondary.
- For a playable implementation, alternate quiet traversal and observation with combat. Threats need clear attack telegraphs when they engage. Directional sound and encounter timing will matter as much as the still image.
