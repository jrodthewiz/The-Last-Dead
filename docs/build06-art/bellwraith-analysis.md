## Layered observation

The dominant macro silhouette is a vertically oriented bell volume with a flared lower mouth and a narrower crown. The lower mouth is an actual negative-space target in the concept and is represented by a separate opening/rim assembly in the runtime model. A smaller skull sits inside the mouth, so the silhouette is compound rather than a single rounded body.

The meso hierarchy is: bell shell, bell rim/opening, skull face, left and right rib appendages, left and right hook hands, membrane sheets, horn crown, chain/fastener rail, ember core, and hover ring. The rib appendages are endpoint-rooted to lateral bell sockets; hooks overlap their hand sockets. The membrane is a two-sided open shell with torn edges and a small amount of procedural flutter.

The material palette is intentionally separated into oxidized bronze (high metalness, satin roughness with patina), dry ivory (near-zero metalness, matte pore response), matte black membrane (double-sided fibrous response), tarnished brass fasteners, soot-dark cavity material, and a hot red emissive core. The concept’s painted illumination is treated as lighting evidence rather than copied into albedo.

Identity features mapped into the spec include bell rim bevel, vertical patina, casting seam, mouth opening, socket gloss, tooth ridge, rib grooves, hook contours, membrane tears, horn ridges/scratches, ember core, chain fasteners, cavity soot, and hover rift.

## Limits and decision

The image does not expose the rear chain layout, bell interior thickness, or animation. Those were recorded as initial unknowns and resolved as explicit stylized assumptions: the rear is mirrored, the membrane is a thin open shell, and pivots are authored for hover/warning/lunge/death readability. This is not a claim of exact hidden-side recovery.

Quality decision: continue into code after the strict spec gate. The procedural factory should be judged on a neutral three-quarter view, a grazing material view, an FPS gameplay view, and an exploded hierarchy view. The 2D concept image is evidence for silhouette/material roles; it is not pixel acceptance authority for 3D realism.
