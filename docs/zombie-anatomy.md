# Zombie anatomy and reanimation

The Ash Witness infected now use targeted anatomical damage rather than random limb rolls. Shots resolve against small capsules derived from the current animated or ragdoll rig; the simulation owns damage, severing, brain integrity and reanimation. The capsules never render, and missing distal sections cease to intercept shots.

## Playing it

- Aim at a leg with the marksman rifle or sustain fire on that limb. Loss of either leg interrupts the current attack and turns the surviving infected into a slower, low crawler with shorter attack reach.
- Aim the Ripper at an arm, leg, head or torso. Its cutting cadence accumulates damage on that particular part. Aim a free F hand slice at an arm beyond grab range, a leg or the upper head; chest-height F in grab range retains the existing committed counter.
- Cuts occur at the aimed point along the limb. Weapon direction and pitch alter the plane. A later, more proximal cut or a different angle can remove another section of the remaining part.
- A crown cut can leave part of the head and viable brain tissue attached. Central head damage, a lower head cut or destruction of the remaining brain permanently stops it. A destroyed brain removes the remaining head section rather than leaving a visibly intact skull.
- A deterministic subset of spawned infected can collapse, become a real ragdoll, then get up once. Bloodhounds have the highest chance. They regain 58% of maximum health, move 36% faster and attack on a shorter cooldown. They remain encounter threats while down, preventing premature room or wave completion. Destroying the brain before they rise prevents revival.
- Fallen bodies remain targetable with guns and the chainsaw. Corpse hits do not award repeated kill credit or heal the player. Extraction is unavailable while a body can still rise.

## Geometry and ownership

The runtime preserves the imported v05 exterior, oral anatomy, textures, skeleton and six clips. Surgery clips posed triangles against a selected body region and an arbitrary plane. It interpolates UVs and bind-space positions, combines and normalizes the strongest four bone weights at new vertices, retains original material groups, and closes boundary loops with separate hard-edge cut surfaces. Detached fragments snapshot the posed imported skin. Head sections show procedural brain folds and a skull rim; limb sections show tissue and bone.

A module worker compiles persistent topology outside the animation loop. The main thread immediately masks the severed surface, shows an offcut made from the same frozen skin, and supplies a temporary closed cap. The live and frozen previews share vertex buffers while retaining separate skeleton matrices; fragment cleanup leaves that geometry with its live owner. Existing skinned bounds and prepared collision envelopes are reused. Worker completion swaps in the closed retained topology and the static offcut without rebuilding the asset. Shader programs are prepared asynchronously for each active play course, including screen and linear bodycam targets, shadow receivers and RGB/RGBA cap variants. Diagnostics expose pending jobs, compiled cuts, cap loops, preparation time and worker failures.

All cuts and brain/revival state are ordinary serialized enemy data, carried by the existing authoritative co-op snapshots. Hit capsules are local, read-only pose inputs. Cosmetic debris uses the bundled Cannon system: 60 Hz fixed stepping, floor/wall colliders, at most four active ragdolls and twelve drops. The shared counter fragment pool stays limited to six rendered fragments; each enemy has eight ordinary cuts plus one reserved terminal head cut, ensuring the budget never prevents visible brain destruction. Restart/course changes release per-actor geometry, preview materials, worker jobs, fragments and physics state.

## Verification ledger

- 134 targeted engine, AI, animation, counter, melee, bite, anatomy and corpse-physics tests passed. Final checks include repeated angled cuts never restoring lost tissue, a reserved visible brain-finishing cut, partial heads remaining targetable without a rendered pose and an aimed F arm slice beyond grab range.
- The real v05 GLB passes cuts at two leg heights, an angled arm, a partial head and an oblique torso. Checks cover closed cap loops, finite geometry, complete vertex attribute coverage including RGBA colors, normalized skin weights, triangle budget and unchanged source geometry.
- All 13 production browser scenarios pass with zero runtime, console, HTTP, failed-request or worker errors. They exercise a real mouse-fired marksman leg shot and keyboard F arm slice, crawling, diagonal arm cuts, partial viable brain, torso bisection, two successive cuts, ragdoll collapse, pose-continuous revival, brain finishing, gore preference and the game's actual restart entrypoint. The 32 anatomy, visual and physics tests also pass after the shared-buffer cleanup change.
- Browser artifacts and the final report are under `output/playwright/enemy-anatomy/production-complete/`. The fixture advances the physics clock, validates every vertex attribute and checks revival begins within 8 cm of the solved ragdoll chest position. The production build includes the anatomy, cut compiler, worker and immediate-preview modules. No Blender conversion or external runtime dependency is required.

Frame pacing is not signed off. Preview preparation took 5.9–35.5 ms in the last headless check, and direct damage calculation took about 0.2 ms. The scene submission still showed first-use spikes: the first completed sever peaked at 568.5 ms and the partial head at 250.7 ms; other cut scenarios peaked at 30–79 ms. These are wall-clock render submission samples on a shared machine under concurrent load, not GPU timings or a controlled frame-rate benchmark. The previous successful run measured a 199 ms first-sever peak. A quiet-machine profile is still needed to separate scheduling, uploads and remaining shader costs and verify the impact frame budget. Functional state coverage alone does not establish smooth 60 FPS.

The severing system is a plane-cut surface system, not a voxel simulation. It deliberately caps complex openings and keeps physics primitive-based, so clothing and soft tissue are visual surfaces rather than independent volumetric organs. Reanimation is a bounded gameplay ability, with one return per eligible infected.
