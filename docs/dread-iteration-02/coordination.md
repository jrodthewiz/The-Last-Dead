## Evidence rules

`before/` preserves the prior pass's captures and diagnostics. `material-check/` is an intermediate source build, not final acceptance. The final gallery distinguishes preliminary and final evidence. The older hit capture froze simulation while advancing the renderer; the new capture advances both at 60 Hz. Therefore the hit pair is useful for appearance, but is not an exact elapsed-time comparison. The old residue view was earlier in collapse; final collapse evidence samples the full clip and includes a changed camera to check corpse heading.

Tests establish state and resource contracts, not subjective visual quality. The visual judge can leave a criterion below target. Short frame samples are not long-session or low-end hardware guarantees.
