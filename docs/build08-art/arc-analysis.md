# Arc image analysis and quality contract

## Intake

Reference: `references/arc-concept-v1.png`.

Suitability is conditional but admitted: the concept is a clean three-quarter product render with a strong long-body silhouette and a visible plasma chamber, grip, trigger frame and claw emitter. The opposite side and inner cage wall are hidden and are documented as authored inference.

## Observation

The macro silhouette is a long mechanical energy rifle. The central identity is the transparent cylindrical reactor with a bright blue-violet core, five clamp rings and a helical conductor. The upper and lower rails carry brass brackets and cable-like supports; the rear has a leather grip and a small bone emblem; the muzzle terminates in a split bone claw.

The meso systems are: a bevelled frame cage with a large center cutout; a glass chamber and inner plasma core; an emissive plasma bead; paired helix conductors; repeated reactor rings; top/bottom rails; mirrored coil guards; a leather grip with brass wraps; and an emitter collar with upper and lower bone claws. Micro details are mapped to rivets, sight blocks, coil pins, wear maps and emissive surfaces.

Materials are inferred as weathered blue-gray gunmetal, warm brass, aged bone, dark leather, blue glass and emissive cyan-violet energy. The energy channels remain independent from the roughness and bump maps.

## Quality contract

The factory must hold the chamber and rails as a non-planar assembly from at least two angles, keep the reactor visible through a real frame opening, provide a readable claw muzzle, and expose named action sockets and an explodable/clickable part hierarchy. The chamber glow is intentionally stylized for the browser and does not claim physically accurate volumetrics.

## Iteration note

The first gameplay camera hid the reactor behind an opaque frame shell and read the proximal stock as a black cylinder. Pass 2 enlarged the receiver opening from `(0.055, 0.08)` to `(0.13, 0.15)`, added an emissive plasma bead, reduced the emitter collar, and moved/scaled the stock to expose the chamber and upper rails.
