# PBR texture credits

These texture sets are CC0 from Poly Haven, copied from the Opus 5.5 HIGHLINE parkour reference's asset set:

- `plaster_grey_04` — Poly Haven, CC0.
- `red_brick_03` — Poly Haven, CC0.
- `asphalt_02` — Poly Haven, CC0.
- `concrete_floor_worn_001` — Poly Haven, CC0.

Each set has diff (sRGB), nor (linear tangent-space normal), and arm (AO in R, roughness in G, metalness in B). They are used as detail layers over the existing vertex-coloured kit, not as a wholesale art-direction replacement.
